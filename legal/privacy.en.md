# Privacy policy

**DRAFT — under review by a lawyer before launch.**

Booga runs a marketplace where private individuals place brands inside content they were going to publish anyway, disclose it as advertising, publish it themselves, and get paid per verified view. This policy sets out exactly what we process, why, and for how long.

Controller: Booga, Stockholm. Contact: hello@joinbooga.se.

## What we collect

- **Account**: mobile number or email, city, age bracket, language.
- **Identity (BankID)**: we **never store your personnummer**. We store a one-way hash of the subject identifier our BankID broker returns, your birth year, and the time of verification. The hash exists to enforce one person per account and to block re-registration after removal.
- **Social accounts**: handle, platform account ID, account type, follower count, average views, categories, and an encrypted access token. We request **read-only access**. We never post, never edit, never follow and never message on your behalf.
- **Placements**: the photo or video you upload, where you chose to place the brand, the versions we generate, your decisions (approved, regenerated, rejected), the link to your published post, and view statistics.
- **Payout**: your Swish number, encrypted.
- **Technical**: IP address, device type and logs, for security and troubleshooting.

## Why we process it

| Purpose | Lawful basis |
|---|---|
| Running the marketplace, paying you | Contract |
| Verifying age and identity | Contract and legal obligation |
| Preventing fraud | Legitimate interest (balancing test documented) |
| Training data for the placement engine | Consent, separate and optional |
| Marketing email | Consent |
| Bookkeeping | Legal obligation |

## Training data

If — and only if — you tick the separate box, we use anonymised versions of your placement choices and images to improve the placement engine. The box is unticked by default, is never bundled with the terms, and can be turned off at any time in Settings. Turning it off stops all future use. Data already anonymised cannot be traced back to you and is retained.

## How long we keep it

| Data | Retention |
|---|---|
| Original images | 90 days after the placement is terminal, unless you gave training consent |
| Verification screenshots | 30 days after the decision |
| View counts | 7 years (bookkeeping support) |
| Ledger and audit log | 7 years (Swedish Bookkeeping Act) |
| BankID hash | Retained after removal, to block re-registration |
| Everything else | Erased on request, after a 30-day grace period |

## What brands see

Brands see aggregated statistics, your handle and the link to your published post — public information. They **never** see your email, phone number, Swish number, age, or any identity data.

## Who we share with

Processors we use: Supabase (database and storage, EU), Vercel (hosting), Stripe (brand payments), Resend (email), PostHog (analytics, EU), Sentry (error reporting), our BankID broker, and Meta (Instagram API). We have a data processing agreement with each.

## Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, and to object. Under **Settings** you can download all of your data and request deletion. Deletion has a 30-day grace period; open placements complete and are paid. Bookkeeping records and the BankID hash are retained per the table above.

You may complain to the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY).

## Cookies

We use necessary cookies for sign-in and language choice, and PostHog for analytics. No advertising pixels.
