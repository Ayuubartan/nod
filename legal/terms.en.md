# Participant terms

**DRAFT — under review by a lawyer before launch.**

This is the agreement between you and Boogaa when you take part in campaigns. We've tried to write it so it can actually be read.

## 1. What Boogaa is

Boogaa brings brand campaigns to you. You choose which ones to take part in. The brand pays Boogaa, and Boogaa pays you. Boogaa keeps the difference — that is how we make money. You never pay Boogaa anything.

## 2. You must be 18

Boogaa is only for people aged 18 and over. We verify this with BankID before your first placement. There is no way around it, including with a guardian's consent.

## 3. What we read from your account

When you connect Instagram or TikTok we request **read-only access**: your profile, your posts and your view statistics. Boogaa never posts for you, never edits your posts, never follows anyone and never sends messages in your name. You can disconnect your account at any time.

## 4. You decide, always

You choose the campaign. You choose where in the image the brand appears. You see the result and approve it before anything is published. You can regenerate, move, swap the product or reject — free of charge. **You publish it yourself.** Boogaa never publishes in your name.

## 5. Disclosure is a condition of payment

Every post must be clearly marked as advertising, using the text the campaign specifies, **first in the caption** — not buried among hashtags. Where the platform offers a paid partnership label, it must be turned on.

A post without correct disclosure **is not paid**. There is no exemption, including from Boogaa's own staff. If disclosure is missing and the post is still live, you get 12 hours to fix it, after which we verify once more.

## 6. How you get paid

- The payout is shown in the campaign before you take it: a fixed amount per approved placement and/or an amount per 1,000 qualified views.
- We verify after a hold period — 24 hours for Stories, 7 days for posts and Reels.
- Qualified views are verified views, after deducting what doesn't count.
- Your payout can never exceed the amount reserved when you claimed the campaign. More views than that is good for the brand, but does not increase the amount.
- Payment is made by Swish within 48 hours of your balance reaching 100 kr.

## 7. Tax

What you earn may be taxable income for you. Boogaa makes no deductions and gives no tax advice, but does send an annual summary of what you were paid.

## 8. Strikes and suspension

| What | Severity |
|---|---|
| Disclosure missing but fixed within the window | Minor |
| Posted late | Minor |
| Post deleted during the hold period | Serious |
| Published content doesn't match what was approved | Serious |
| Confirmed fraud | Serious |

One serious strike or three minor ones leads to a 90-day suspension. Your balance is paid out. Repeat offences lead to account removal.

## 9. If you want to stop

You can stop at any time. Open placements complete and are paid. Under Settings you can download your data and request deletion, with a 30-day grace period.

## 10. Liability

Boogaa is not responsible for how your audience reacts, for the platforms' decisions about your account, or for payment withheld when a post does not meet the conditions above. We are responsible for paying what you have earned under the campaign's terms.

## 11. Changes and disputes

We give advance notice of material changes. Swedish law applies. Disputes are heard by Swedish courts; you may also contact the National Board for Consumer Disputes (ARN).
